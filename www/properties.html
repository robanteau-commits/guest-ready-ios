    6. Calendar sync (optional)

    Connect your listing calendars so Guest Ready can pull in your booking dates automatically. You can skip this and add it later from property settings.

    How to find your Airbnb iCal link

    1. Log into Airbnb and go to Calendar
    2. Click Availability settings (top right)
    3. Scroll to Sync calendars → Export calendar
    4. Copy the link and paste it above
    Open Airbnb help article →

    How to find your Vrbo iCal link

    1. Log into Vrbo and go to My listings
    2. Select your property → Calendar
    3. Click Import/export → Export your calendar
    4. Copy the link and paste it above
    Open Vrbo help article →
    💡

    Calendar sync is optional but recommended — it prevents double-booking by importing your reservation dates automatically.
